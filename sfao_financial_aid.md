**Last verified:** 19 August 2026

### General Eligibility for University of Ghana Financial Aid

To qualify to apply for and receive financial aid from the University of Ghana, a student must meet all of the following requirements:

1. Be a Ghanaian citizen
2. Be enrolled as a regular student in a full time programme of study
3. Be able to demonstrate financial need
4. Be brilliant (strong academic standing)
5. Be making excellent academic progress as determined by the University

Financial aid provided by the University covers academic fees only. Additional elements are added as resources become available. Financial aid takes the form of a full scholarship, a partial scholarship, fee payment arrangements, or on campus work study or part time job opportunities.

The application process for continuing students commences in November each year. Awards are made by the end of the second semester, to be used in the following academic year.

## [DOCUMENT_ID: SFAO_POLICY_2026_UGBS_SCHOLARS_SCHOLARSHIP]

**Source:** https://www.ug.edu.gh/financialaid/node/62
**Last verified:** 19 August 2026

### UGBS Scholars' Scholarship, 2026/2027

Provides financial support to exceptional undergraduate students from disadvantaged backgrounds at the University of Ghana Business School, Main Campus.

**Eligibility**
1. Must be a UGBS student, BSc Business Administration only
2. Must be able to demonstrate limited and/or insufficient family income to cover most or all educational related expenses
3. Must show the will to succeed (determination, perseverance and success in other pursuits)
4. Must be a continuing student, Level 200 and upward, with a CGPA of 3.0 or better

**Coverage**
The scholarship provides financial support toward tuition fees. As with general SFAO aid, coverage is for academic fees, not accommodation, unless otherwise stated in a specific award letter.

**How to apply**
1. Visit https://www.ug.edu.gh/financialaid/
2. Go to Scholarships, then UGBS Scholars' Scholarship
3. Read the eligibility criteria and complete the online application form, including the required essays
4. Print a hard copy of the declaration form and submit it to SFAO by the deadline

**Deadline (2026/2027 cycle):** Wednesday, 29 July 2026, 3:00pm

**Required documents:** Admission letter, proof of registration, academic records (WASSCE grades or UG transcripts), letters of recommendation, birth certificates of siblings, evidence of parent or guardian income, and any other supporting documents.

> Note: the scholarship page explicitly states that applicants are advised against using AI generated essays.

**Contact for this scholarship:** 0303-965240 or 0206804077, or finaid@ug.edu.gh. Location: GCB Building near 'N' Block.

## [DOCUMENT_ID: SFAO_POLICY_2026_SRC_YIBIBOA]

**Source:** https://www.ug.edu.gh/financialaid/node/27
**Last verified:** 19 August 2026

### SRC Yi Bi Boa Scholarship Scheme

A scholarship scheme run by the UGSRC (Students' Representative Council) to provide financial support to exceptional undergraduate students with higher financial needs, across the University of Ghana (not UGBS specific).

**Eligibility**
1. Must be an SRC dues paying student of the University of Ghana (regular, fee paying, city campus, or distance education)
2. Must be able to demonstrate financial need
3. Must be a continuing student with a CGPA of 1.5 or better

**How to apply**
1. Visit https://www.ug.edu.gh/financialaid/
2. Go to Scholarships, then SRC Yi Bi Boa Scholarship Scheme
3. Complete the online application form with required essays and documents
4. Print a hard copy of the declaration form and submit it to SFAO by the deadline
5. New applicants select 'New Application Submission'; those renewing select 'Renewal of Application'

**Deadline (2026/2027 cycle):** Thursday, 2 July 2026

**Contact for this scheme:** 0302-945312 / 0205-186904 / 0206804077, or finaid@ug.edu.gh. Location: GCB Building near 'N' Block.

### Work Study and Part Time Job Opportunities

The SFAO's own overview page lists "on campus work study or part time job opportunities" as one of the recognised forms of financial aid, alongside full scholarships, partial scholarships and fee arrangements. However, at the time of writing, the public SFAO website does not carry a dedicated page describing a standalone work study application process, eligibility criteria, job listings, or hours.

**Recommendation for the prototype:** Do not invent specific eligibility rules, hourly rates, or an application procedure for work study, since none of this is publicly documented. The safest grounded answer for the chatbot is to confirm that work study exists as a form of aid and to direct the student to contact SFAO directly for current openings and criteria, rather than generating plausible sounding but unverified details.

**Suggested Q&A answer:** "Work study and part time job opportunities are one of the forms of financial aid SFAO offers, but current openings and specific eligibility rules are not published online. Please contact SFAO directly on 0205-186904 or finaid@ug.edu.gh to ask about current work study opportunities."

## Question and Answer Pairs

**Q: Does the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship cover accommodation?**
A: The scholarship page does not explicitly separate tuition from accommodation coverage the way some other scholarships do. What is confirmed is that it provides financial support to exceptional undergraduate BSc Business Administration students from disadvantaged backgrounds, and that SFAO aid generally covers academic fees, with additional elements added as resources allow. For a definite answer on whether your specific award includes accommodation, contact SFAO directly.

**Q: Who can apply for the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship?**
A: You must be a UGBS student on the BSc Business Administration programme, a continuing student at Level 200 or above with a CGPA of 3.0 or better, able to show limited or insufficient family income to cover your education costs, and demonstrate the will to succeed.

**Q: What is the deadline for the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship?**
A: For the 2026/2027 cycle, the deadline is Wednesday, 29 July 2026 at 3:00pm. Deadlines change each cycle, so always confirm the current date on the SFAO website before applying.

**Q: Can a Level 100 student apply for the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship?**
A: No. The scholarship requires a continuing student at Level 200 or above with a CGPA of 3.0 or better, so first year students are not eligible for this particular scheme.

**Q: What is the SRC Yi Bi Boa Scholarship and who can apply?**
A: It is a scholarship scheme run by the SRC to support exceptional undergraduate students across the whole University of Ghana with higher financial needs. To apply you must be an SRC dues paying student, able to demonstrate financial need, and a continuing student with a CGPA of 1.5 or better.

**Q: What is the difference between the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship and the SRC Yi Bi Boa Scheme?**
A: The UGBS Scholars' Scholarship is specific to BSc Business Administration students at UGBS and requires a CGPA of 3.0 or better. The SRC Yi Bi Boa Scheme is open to SRC dues paying students across the whole university and only requires a CGPA of 1.5 or better, so it has a lower academic bar but is not UGBS specific.

**Q: Can I get help paying my fees in instalments?**
A: Fee payment arrangements are one of the recognised forms of SFAO support, but the specific instalment terms are not published online. Contact SFAO directly to discuss what arrangement might be possible for your situation.

**Q: Are there part time jobs available through SFAO?**
A: On campus work study or part time job opportunities are listed as one of SFAO's forms of aid, but current openings, hours and eligibility are not published on the website. Contact SFAO directly for up to date information.

**Q: How do I apply for SFAO scholarships?**
A: Visit the SFAO website, go to the Scholarships menu, select the specific scholarship you are eligible for, read its eligibility criteria, and complete the online application form with any required essays. Most schemes also require you to print and submit a hard copy declaration form to the SFAO office by the deadline.

**Q: What documents do I need for the UGBS Scholars' Scholarship?**
A: Admission letter, proof of registration, academic records (WASSCE grades or UG transcripts), letters of recommendation, birth certificates of siblings, evidence of your parent or guardian's income, and any other documents that support your application.

**Q: How do I contact the Students Financial Aid Office?**
A: Call 0205-186904, 0206804077, or 0303-965240, or email finaid@ug.edu.gh. The office is at House No. 11, East Legon, Main Campus (also described as the GCB Building near 'N' Block on some scholarship pages), or you can walk in during working hours.

**Q: I am not a Ghanaian citizen, can I apply for SFAO financial aid?**
A: General SFAO eligibility requires the applicant to be a Ghanaian citizen. If you are an international student facing financial difficulty, SFAO's general schemes are unlikely to apply to you, and you should ask the International Programmes Office or your college directly about support options, since this was not confirmed as within SFAO's remit.
