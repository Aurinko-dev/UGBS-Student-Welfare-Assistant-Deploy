# Graduation, Certificates and Transcripts (STS FAQs)

Source: sts.ug.edu.gh FAQ page, summarised from screenshots. [Verify against sts.ug.edu.gh and Academic Affairs before treating as final.] Some answers are written for College of Humanities students, including distance learners.

## Graduation

### Q: How do I know if I have satisfied all the requirements for graduation?
A: The requirements are in the Regulations for Junior Members. A soft copy is on the University website. You can also contact the Academic Affairs office of your college.

### Q: How do I know I am eligible to graduate?
A: A student who has satisfied all departmental requirements and passed all UGRCs is eligible. Section 9.30 of the Regulations for Junior Members sets out the requirements.

### Q: How many credit hours do I need to pass to be eligible for graduation?
A: A minimum of 120 credit hours for BA, BSc Administration and LLB. BFA students need a minimum of 131. (Regulations p. 49.)

### Q: How many total credit hours do I need to take to be eligible for graduation?
A: A minimum of 120 credit hours.

### Q: Can I graduate with an F in an elective course under the College of Humanities?
A: Yes, but not in every department. Students offering Economics cannot graduate with an F in any of the courses. In other departments, you can graduate with an F in any elective course as long as your total Es and Fs do not exceed 16 credit hours. (Regulations p. 49.)

### Q: Can I graduate with an E in a core course in the College of Humanities?
A: Yes. Apart from UGRCs, where students can only graduate with D and above, students in the college can graduate with an E in any core course, provided total Es and Fs do not exceed 16 credit hours. (Regulations p. 49.)

### Q: I satisfied all requirements but my name is not on the graduating list.
A: Contact your Head of Department or Examinations Officer for assistance.

### Q: I have written all my re-sit courses. How can I be added to the graduation list?
A: Present a current copy of your academic records to the Academic Affairs Office for processing for the subsequent graduation period.

### Q: I completed my course of study last year with some outstanding results. They are now entered but my name isn't on this year's graduating list.
A: Print your academic records and submit them at the Academic Affairs office of the college. They will process it for you.

### Q: Can I be part of the graduation ceremony after completing school when I have passed all re-sit papers?
A: Yes, provided your last registration is the same as that of the cohort graduating.

### Q: What should I do when my name does not appear correctly for the online registration for graduation?
A: Visit the University website and use the Congregation link for all enquiries.

### Q: If I fail to participate in matriculation and fail to sign the matriculation oath, will I be allowed to graduate?
A: Answer not captured (see section 11).

## Certificates and Transcripts

### Q: I graduated last year but did not attend the congregation. How do I get my certificate?
A: Pay the appropriate fee at the main University Cash Office (in the Registry), then go to the Academic Affairs Directorate with your student ID card to collect your certificate.

### Q: Can I ask someone to pick up my certificate on my behalf?
A: No, students must pick up their certificates in person.

### Q: I am out of the country but need my certificate. Can I send someone to collect it?
A: A letter of authorization signed by the student can be given to someone to collect it. This is solely at the discretion of the Director of Academic Affairs.

### Q: Can I give an authorization note to a relation to collect my BSc certificate?
A: No. The University will normally not release the certificate to anyone other than the graduand. In exceptional cases, for example where the student is outside the country, an exception may be requested.

Note: answers on collection by another person vary. The default is in person; exceptions are at the discretion of the Director of Academic Affairs. Please confirm with them.

### Q: I have misplaced my BSc certificate. Can I request a replacement?
A: The University issues certificates once per graduating student. If a certificate is missing, the student is given an attestation.

### Q: How can I get my transcript and certified copies of my certificate?
A: They are issued at the Academic Affairs Directorate on payment of the appropriate fees.

## Q: Where can I view my transcript online?
A: Log into MIS Web at https://mis.ug.edu.gh to access your academic transcript, results, and student record. You will need your Student ID and MIS PIN to log in. You can also access transcript requests through the STS portal at https://sts.ug.edu.gh.

## Q: What is DiGradSys and how do I use it?
A: DiGradSys is the University of Ghana's digital graduation system. Use it to:
1. Check if your name is on the graduation list: https://sts.ug.edu.gh/services/graduation/search
2. Register for congregation: https://sts.ug.edu.gh/services/graduation/login (requires Student ID and MIS PIN)
3. Check your graduation status and outstanding requirements: https://graduation.ug.edu.gh/uggraduation/
Clear all financial obligations before registering. Congregation fees are GHS 540.00, payable at the Students Accounts Office in the Jones-Quartey Building (JQB).

## Q: Where is the Students Accounts Office?
A: The Students Accounts Office is in the Jones-Quartey Building (JQB), ground floor, Main Campus. Go here for fee payments, student account issues, refunds, and fee balances. Email: studentsaccounts@ug.edu.gh. Payment hours: 9:00am - 4:00pm.

## Q: How do I access my exam timetable on DiGradSys?
A: 1. Open the DiGradSys portal using Safari or Chrome. 2. Log in with your student email (use your Student ID as the password if this is your first login). 3. Once logged in, go to: Timetable → Exam Timetable → Display Exam Timetable.

## Q: What is DiGradSys?
A: DiGradSys (UG-DiGradSys) is the University of Ghana's official digitized academic and graduation portal. It handles exam timetables, seat allocations and venues, graduation eligibility and clearance tracking, and attendance records. It is used across the University, including by UGBS students.

## Q: How do I log in to DiGradSys?
A: 1. Go to the official UG-DiGradSys login page. 2. Enter your full UG student email as the username (e.g., username@st.ug.edu.gh). 3. Enter your Student ID number as the password. 4. Alternatively, use the "Login with UG Mail" or "Login with Microsoft" button to sign in with your standard university email credentials.

## Q: Where do I check my graduation requirements and clearance status?
A: This is done through DiGradSys, which evaluates your academic record against graduation requirements and manages the clearance process needed to join the congregation (graduation) ceremony.
