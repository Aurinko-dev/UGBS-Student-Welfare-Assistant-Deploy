# University of Ghana — Academic Affairs Directorate

## Frequently Asked Questions and Answers

Source: University of Ghana Academic Affairs Directorate Q&A dataset, compiled from official UG Admissions and Academic Affairs webpages (ug.edu.gh). [VERIFIED]


## Before Admission

### Q: When can I apply for the next academic year?
A: Applications are usually opened by the month of March within the year of intended entry! Prospective students are encouraged to visit the official website at Welcome! | UG Admissions to read all guidelines before starting their application.

### Q: How do I apply for the University of Ghana?
A: Start at the UG Admissions site, follow the “How to Apply” steps (e‑voucher → form), then submit via the Application Portal and later check your Admission Status. UG Admissions (overview) How to Apply (Undergraduate) Application Portal (login) Admission Status Checker

### Q: Where can I find the Academic Calendar?
A: Use the Academic Calendar hub for undergraduate key dates (with downloadable PDFs). For graduate dates, see the Graduate Academic Calendar. Academic Calendar (Undergraduate hub) Academic Calendar (Graduate)

### Q: I am a former student of UG. Do I need a new ID number?
A: If you attended or graduated with a Certificate/Diploma from UG in the past, you must provide your original Student Identity Number on your application. This number will remain the same if you are re-admitted.

### Q: What are the most recent WAEC WASSCE aggregate cut-offs for admission into various programmes of study in UG?
A: Cut-off points vary each year; check the Admissions Criteria for details: Cut Off Points | UG Admissions .

### Q: How can I get support for my application and admission enquiries
A: To get support for your application and admission inquiries at the University of Ghana, you can: Browse the admissions webpages on our official website: The pages are loaded with answers and should be your first point of contact. email admissions office via admissions@ug.edu.gh Call the phone helpline or, visit our campus office at the Registry of the Great Hall (outside the admissions period) or at the Admissions Assistance Desks at the UG Stadium (during the admissions period) for direct assistance.

### Q: What should I do if I have not received my admission letter?
A: Check the application portal; if unresolved, contact the Admissions Office. During the admissions period, support will be available at the Admissions Assistance Desk, usually located at the UG Stadium. Please note that acceptance to the University is based on whether your programme choices meet the admission cut-offs for that year. That is why applicants are given the opportunity to change their selection before closure of the applications portal.

### Q: Where can international students obtain admission support?
A: Visit the International Programmes Page s, vital information or contact international admissions at international@ug.edu.gh or admissions@ug.edu.gh .

### Q: How do I transfer from another university to UG?
A: Write to admissions@ug.edu.gh providing the relevant official academic records from your home university. This should be done three months before the start of the academic year you intend to enrol.

### Q: Which programmes must be selected as a "First Choice" only?
A: To avoid disqualification due to high competitiveness, the following programmes of interest must advisedly be selected as first choice: All College of Health Sciences programmes (excluding School of Biomedical and Allied Health Sciences). College of Basic and Applied Sciences: Doctor of Veterinary Medicine, B.Sc. Information Technology, B.Sc. Computer Engineering, and B.Sc. Biomedical Engineering. College of Humanities: B.Sc. Administration or Bachelor of Laws (LLB) This is just a guide as the competiveness of programmes of study may vary from year to year.

### Q: What should I choose as a second choice if I apply for Law (LLB) or Administration?
A: Applicants for B.Sc. Administration or LLB are strongly advised to select Bachelor of Arts (BA) subject bouquets as their second choice to increase their chances of admission.

### Q: How do programmes that run the subject bouquet system run?  How does the subject selection work??
A: You must select up to four (4) subject bouquets in order of preference, depending on the programme. Once enrolled, you will study three subjects at Level 100, narrowing down to one or two subjects by Level 400.

### Q: What is the "Fee-paying Scheme"?
A: If you qualify for a degree at the Legon Campus but do not meet the competitive cut-off point for your preferred programme, you may opt for the Fee-paying Scheme. You must indicate your interest in this option directly on your application form. The academic fees are higher than regular fees, but still comparatively subsidised. Please note that there are approved cut-offs for fee-paying status as well.

### Q: Are there options for Mature Students?
A: Yes. The Mature Students Admission pathway is available for the Bachelor of Arts Distance Education (DE) programme. Registration is opened and advertised on the UG Website for the University of Ghana mature access course several times a year. Successful applicants will be admitted into the Level 100 (Bachelor of Arts) or Diploma Distance Education Programmes based on their performance in the entrance examinations Entry requirements: Applicants must be at least 25 years old at the time of application and show proof of age with the ECOWAS/National ID Card or any legitimate documentary proof of date of birth which is at least 5 years old. Applicants must register for, attend and complete the University of Ghana Access Course and pass the Mature Students’ Entrance Examination conducted by the University. Applicants who pass the Entrance Examination must purchase an e-voucher from designated banks to access the UG online application form. Please note that this is the official University of Ghana Mature Access Course. All other advertised courses have not been sanctioned by the University. Only candidates who participate in the official University of Ghana Mature Access Course will be eligible to take the entrance examination.

### Q: Can someone help me secure admission for a fee?
A: No. The University of Ghana admission process is strictly transparent. Beware of individuals claiming they can assist with admission for a fee; these are fraudulent claims.


## After Admission Offer

### Q: How do I access the Schedule of Fees?
A: See AAD’s Schedule of Fees page for academic and residential fees; programme‑specific fee schedules are linked as PDFs. Schedule of Fees (AAD)

### Q: Where can I pay my academic fees?
A: Specific information on payment points is provided with the admission letter. Payments can be made online through the UG Payments Portal or at designated banks on and off campus. Please note that payment points vary for different programmes of study.

### Q: What should I do after paying my fees?
A: Obtain and keep the payment receipt; Wait for your account to be cleared within 48 hours and then register promptly. To resolve any related problems, proceed to the Students Accounts Office with proof of payment and admission documents, for support or email them at studentsaccounts@ug.edu.gh .

### Q: How do I register for courses?
A: Register within the calendar windows via STS (login) and check add/drop and re‑sit registration windows on the Academic Calendar. STS Student Services (login) Academic Calendar (registration windows) For support regarding registration challenges, contact the relevant College Academic Office before the course registration deadline.

### Q: I cannot register my courses; how do I go about it?
A: Kindly visit your College Academic Officer promptly before the course registration deadline and talk to the academic officers for assistance, they are more capable to help.

### Q: How do I correct biographic data errors on my admission letter?
A: Make sure you are fully enrolled and then notify the Academic Affairs Directorate via your official UG student email account before Matriculation at aad@ug.edu.gh with supporting documents.

### Q: Are installment payment options available?
A: Yes, subject to approval; the University has a credit policy. In good time, before that deadline of course registration, send an official email request using your official student email account to the Director, Academic Affairs at aad@ug.edu.gh for consideration. After that deadline, this cannot be supported and students will be required to request for deferment of their programme on the STS portal.

### Q: What happens if I miss the admission enrolment deadline?
A: Your offer will be forfeited.

### Q: What should I do if I have paid and now need to withdraw from the programme of study?
A: This request can only be supported before the deadline indicated in the admission letter . Fill the Refund Request Form on the STS portal, print out and sign.  Using your UG student email account, email the form with a request for withdrawal & refund, payment receipts, and a copy of the admission letter before that deadline to the Director, Academic Affairs at aad@ug.edu.gh .


## During the Semester

### Q: Who do I contact for a missing grade?
A: If you are a student at the University of Ghana and you have a missing grade, here are the steps you can take to address the issue: Check Your Records: First, verify your grades through the university’s online student portal or student information system to confirm that the grade is indeed missing or has not just been updated. Contact Your Course Lecturer /Examiner: Reach out to the lecturer of the course where the grade is missing. They may have the most up-to-date information or can explain any delays in posting grades. You can write them an email or visit their office in the Department. Visit the Department or Faculty Office: If the Lecturer cannot resolve the issue, contact the Head of department or Departmental Examinations Officer for that course. They can resolve the matter, guide you on the next steps or help facilitate communication. Submit a Formal Request: The University has a formal process for resolving grade issues. If the grade issue is still not resolved, write a formal email addressed to the Director, Academic Affairs, explaining your situation clearly. Include your student ID, course code, semester, and any relevant details.. Follow Up: Keep track of your request and follow up if you do not receive a response within a reasonable time frame. Patience and persistence are key. Seek Assistance from College Academic Offices: If needed, you can also approach the College/SGS Academic Officers for advice and assistance in navigating the process. Remember : Always maintain a respectful and polite tone when communicating. Clearly state your concern, provide all necessary details, and ask for guidance on how to resolve the missing grade issue.

### Q: How do I check the examination timetable?
A: Examination periods are listed on the Academic Calendar; exam resources are gathered under AAD Resources at Assessments and Examinations | Academic Affairs . Other links: Academic Calendar (exam windows) Final Undergraduate Examinations Timetable Portal On the Examinations Portal.

### Q: Where can I access the official lecture timetable and locations for classes for my registered courses?
A: Visit the relevant Departments to check the teaching timetables from the Notice Boards and General Offices. You may also receive that information on the SAKAI page of the courses.

### Q: What should I do if there are clashes in my lecture timetable?
A: Report to the relevant Head of Department or Examinations Officer for resolution.

### Q: What should I do if there are clashes in my final examinations timetable or if my examination is not listed?
A: Notify the Teaching and Examinations Unit (Room D7) of the Academic Affairs Directorate promptly via your UG student email account at teu-academic@ug.edu.gh .

### Q: How do I confirm my examination venue?
A: Check the daily timetables for Final Examinations here: https://sts.ug.edu.gh/timetable/

### Q: What should I do if my name is not on the class attendance list?
A: If your name is not on the class attendance list, it may indicate that you have not registered for the course; please review your registration proof and register if registration is still open. If registration is closed, such students will be ineligible to take the final exam and will be permitted to undertake the outstanding course at the next available opportunity on request to the Director, AAD at aad@ug.edu.gh .

### Q: What should I do if I miss the final examination for a course?
A: If you miss the final exams for a course, you will receive a grade of 'X' for the course and will be able to register to undertake and write examinations for that course at the next available opportunity. Students who miss examinations due to ill health may however send an email with an official medical report to aad@ug.edu.gh before the start of the next semester. Such students will be awarded a grade I.

### Q: Who do I contact regarding continuous assessment or coursework issues?
A: Contact the course lecturer/examiner or Head of Department.

### Q: How can I track my examinations malpractice case for the outcome
A: Check for a push notification on the case via the STS portal. You may also use your official UG student email account to send an email to bcsecretariat@ug.edu.gh providing the necessary details to enable a check to be undertaken.

## Academic Progression

### Q: What is the maximum credit load allowed per semester?
A: The standard maximum varies by programme of study.  Check of the Students Handbook for the maximum and minimum for your programme: Student Handbook | University of Ghana

### Q: How do I apply for an extension of credit hours (credit overload)?
A: Submit an emailed official request through the Head of Department to the Dean of the School, supported by academic performance evidence and provide details of course(s) to be added.

### Q: Under what circumstances are students granted credit overload?
A: Typically, students with excellent academic records and good attendance are considered.  It is recommended that students performing badly do not apply.

### Q: Will taking extra credits affect my academic standing?
A: If approved, it should not negatively affect your standing; however, students must maintain good academic performance.

### Q: What should I do if my course results are not showing?
A: For Core and Elective courses as well as HERCs courses, contact the Examiner for the course directly for support. The Head of the Academic Department may be contacted if the delay extends. For UGRC courses, Contact the Coordinator UGRCs for support at ugrc@ug.edu.gh for support.

### Q: How do I resolve any challenges with registering my courses as an amnesty student?
A: Kindly open the amnesty advert on the University’s website and follow the instructions. If you have any challenges, please contact the relevant College Academic Office for assistance.

## Graduation and Congregation

### Q: Where do I find graduation eligibility, schedules and the graduation list?
A: See AAD’s Graduation & Congregation hub for eligibility and schedules; when released, check your name on the STS Graduation List. Graduation & Congregation (AAD) Graduation List (STS)

### Q: My name is not on the graduation list — where do I seek assistance?
A: If your name is not on the graduation list at the University of Ghana, you should take the following steps to seek assistance: Verify Your Graduation Requirements: Ensure that you have completed all academic and administrative requirements for graduation, such as completing all courses, clearing fees, and submitting necessary documentation. Log into the DiGradSys for support in this regard: Check for Outstanding Requirements: Ensure you have settled all financial obligations, cleared any academic holds, or completed any administrative steps needed for graduation. Check with Your Department or College: Visit or contact the Departmental Examinations Officer responsible for your programme. They can confirm whether your name has been forwarded for graduation and if there are any outstanding issues. Contact the College/SGS Academic Office: Reach out directly to the College Academic Office. They manage the graduation list and can provide specific information about your status. They can check your academic records and help resolve the issue. Submit a Formal Inquiry: Write a formal email to the Director of Academic Affairs, explaining your situation. Include your student ID, program, year of graduation, and any relevant details. Request clarification on why your name is not on the list and ask for guidance on the next steps. Follow Up: Stay in touch and follow up regularly until the issue is resolved.

A: Gowns are collected at the relevant collection points during specified dates before ceremonies: Collection of Gowns | Academic Affairs

### Q: What are the requirements for gown collection?

### Q: What happens if the gown is lost or damaged?

## Useful Extras

### Q: Where can I see all academic services in one place?
A: Use the AAD Resources & Services gateway for Registration, Academic Support, Exams and Graduation. AAD Resources & Services

### Q: How do I communicate with the Academic Affairs Directorate for best results
A: Tips for Efficient Communication AAD prefers emailed digital communication for efficiency and record-keeping, requiring students or staff to use their official @st.ug.edu.gh or @ug.edu.gh email addresses, include clear subjects, relevant details ( ID, College & Programme ), and attachments, and use the same previous email trails for follow-ups. Additionally, users should consult the FAQs for common queries. Remember: Remain polite and concise in your communication, providing all relevant details to facilitate quick assistance.

### Q: How can I rent an academic facility for our student organisation event/ programme?
A: Write officially in good time to the Dean of Students for permission to organise the event giving the purpose. Indicate the venue needed, as well as the date and time for the event. Following approval of the event, the Dean of Students will forward the request to the Director of Academic Affairs for consideration of allocation of the venue indicated based on its availability. Feedback will be sent via email . (In this regard, ensure that the recipient email is provided in the letter of request) The University charges a subsidized rent to registered students' organizations for use of specific facilities. Please note that not all facilities are available for rent.

### Q: I am a Course Rep. How can I request for space for tutorials or extra tuition for my class?
A: Write officially via your official student email address to the Director of Academic Affairs at aad@ug.edu.gh for support. Indicate the course code, title and time slot needed. The Examiner for the course should be copied in the email. Please note that provision is subject to the availability of time slot or space within the venue requested.
