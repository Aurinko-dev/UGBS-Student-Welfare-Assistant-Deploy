# UGBS Academic Policies & Support (Official Q&A for RAG)

### Q: How do continuing UGBS students request course deferment?
A: Log into the STS Portal, go to Services, select Deferment Form, complete and submit the application within the approved period. Deferment requests are processed through Academic Affairs after departmental consideration.

### Q: How do first-year (freshmen) students apply for deferment?
A: Freshmen submit a written application to the Academic Affairs Directorate through the official Academic Affairs process. Applications may be submitted through the official Academic Affairs email and the procedures published by Academic Affairs.

### Q: What is the maximum deferment period?
A: Students may defer their programme for a maximum of two consecutive semesters (one academic year), subject to University approval.

### Q: How do I resume after deferment?
A: Submit a resumption request through the procedures indicated in the deferment approval letter and complete registration within the approved registration period.

### Q: What are the normal semester credit limits?
A: A maximum of 17 credit hours is required by students offering B.A., BSc Administration, and LLB students. Students offering B.F.A can take up to a maximum of 21 credit hours (Refer to page 42 of Regulations for Junior Members, Vol. 1; September 2016).

### Q: What is a credit overload?
A: A credit overload is registration above the approved maximum semester credit load.

### Q: How is a credit overload approved?
A: The student applies for approval. The request requires approval by the Head of Department and the Dean before registration is finalized.

### Q: Can I add or drop courses after registration?
A: Yes. Add/drop is only permitted during the official add/drop period announced by Academic Affairs.

### Q: Who is my academic advisor?
A: Academic advising is coordinated through each UGBS department. Students should contact their department office for their assigned advisor.

### Q: Which UGBS departments provide academic advising?
A: Accounting, Finance, Marketing and Entrepreneurship, Operations and Management Information Systems (OMIS), Public Administration and Health Services Management, and Organization and Human Resource Management.

### Q: Where do I seek departmental academic support?
A: Contact your department office or departmental academic advisor during official office hours.

### Q: What should I do if my Continuous Assessment (CA) score is missing?
A: First contact the course lecturer or course examiner with evidence. If unresolved, escalate through the Head of Department according to University procedures.

### Q: What if my quiz or assignment marks are incorrect?
A: Raise the issue promptly with the lecturer and provide supporting evidence before results are finalized.

### Q: How do I request tutorial or class support?
A: Contact the course lecturer or department office for information about tutorial arrangements and available class spaces.

### Q: Is welfare support available for students in financial hardship?
A: Students experiencing exceptional personal or financial hardship may contact the Office of the Dean of UGBS for guidance on available hardship or emergency support procedures.

### Q: I am struggling financially and falling behind in my courses at the same time — what should I do?
A: Handle the two problems in parallel. For the fees, contact the Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO) about hardship support, and the Office of the Dean of UGBS for the University's hardship procedures. For the coursework, tell your lecturer(s) and departmental academic advisor now — the earlier you raise it, the more options remain (tutorial support, a CA review, or deferment). If both are serious, the Office of the Dean of UGBS can help coordinate support across both.
