# University of Ghana — Student Accommodation

Source: University of Ghana Academic Affairs Directorate, official Accommodation page (ug.edu.gh/aad/accomodation) and Schedule of Fees page (ug.edu.gh/aad/schedule-fees). Verified against the live pages, word-for-word match. [VERIFIED]

## Overview
On-campus accommodation is not automatically included with admission. Spaces are limited and allocated through a competitive online system. Students are strongly advised to use only authorized University platforms for accommodation arrangements and to avoid making payments to third-party individuals or agents — the University does not recognize or endorse external accommodation services. Accommodation on the Legon campus is provided subject to availability, under the University's Open Random Bed Allocation system, held before the start of each academic year.

## Q: Am I eligible for on-campus housing?
A: Only enrolled University of Ghana students are eligible for on-campus housing during the academic year.

## Q: How does the random bed allocation process work?
A: Students must complete academic registration first before expressing interest in the random bed assignment. To participate, opt in via the official STS Portal: https://sts.ug.edu.gh. The University announces the opening and deadline for submitting interest each year — check the portal and official announcements for current dates. The allocation itself is conducted online, openly and transparently, so stakeholders can observe the process.

## Q: Is on-campus accommodation guaranteed?
A: No. On-campus accommodation is not guaranteed with admission. Students who do not secure on-campus housing must make alternative arrangements. The University continues to expand residential facilities, but space remains limited.

## Q: Can I pay someone directly to secure a bed?
A: No — this is strictly prohibited. No payments should be made to any individual or third party for beds; this is illegal, and both the buyer and the seller face sanctions. "Perching" (unauthorized residence in a hall or room you were not assigned) is also strictly prohibited and results in disciplinary action.

## Q: What are the Traditional Halls?
A: The University's traditional halls are Akuafo, Legon, Volta, Commonwealth, and Mensah Sarbah. Each has its own history, culture, and traditions.

## Q: What are the UGEL Hostels?
A: The UGEL residences are Hilla Limann, Kwapong, Elizabeth Sey, and Jean Nelson — more modern halls with amenities like internet access and laundry facilities.

## Q: What can I do about a problem with my roommate?
A: Note: this answer is not from the official Accommodation or Schedule of Fees pages cited above — it reflects general University practice for resolving issues once you already have a room, as distinct from getting one assigned in the first place.

If you have a problem with a roommate (for example, a serious disagreement, a shared-space issue, or a safety concern), you do not go through the STS Portal or the random bed allocation process for this -- that process only covers getting a room assigned. Instead:

1. **Talk to your roommate directly first**, if it feels safe to do so -- many issues are simple misunderstandings that can be resolved this way.
2. **If that doesn't work, contact your Hall Tutor** (for a Traditional Hall: Akuafo, Legon, Volta, Commonwealth, or Mensah Sarbah) or the **hostel's own management office** (for a UGEL Hostel: Hilla Limann, Kwapong, Elizabeth Sey, or Jean Nelson; or for a private hostel around Legon). The Hall Tutor or hostel management can mediate the issue, and can reassign a room in serious cases.
3. **Check your hall or hostel's own rules and regulations**, usually published on its section of the University of Ghana website or given to you at check-in, since some halls and hostels have their own specific process for handling roommate disputes, noise complaints, or shared-space conduct.
4. If the issue involves harassment, threats, or violence rather than an ordinary disagreement, this is a safety matter, not just an accommodation matter -- see the sexual harassment support document, or speak to your Hall Tutor/hostel management and CEGENSA together.

A roommate conflict is different from the accommodation-allocation questions elsewhere in this document, and going to the wrong office (e.g. asking the STS Portal to fix a roommate problem) will not resolve it.

## Q: Where do I find residential fees?
A: Fees differ by hall type and by whether a student is Ghanaian or an international student. Direct sources:
- Traditional Halls fee schedule: https://www.ug.edu.gh/aad/sites/academic.ug.edu.gh/files/2026-01/2025_26%20TRADITIONAL%20HALLS%20Fees%20%281%29.pdf

- General Schedule of Fees page (all categories, updated each academic year): https://www.ug.edu.gh/aad/schedule-fees
- Bank/payment details differ for Ghanaian vs. International students — see the payment info links on the Schedule of Fees page.

## Q: What's the minimum payment needed before I can register for accommodation?
A: Per the Schedule of Fees page: 100% of the residential fee must be paid before registration for the semester (this is separate from academic fee registration thresholds, which are at least 50% for First Semester and 100% for Second Semester).

## Note on nationality
Academic and residential fees vary for Ghanaian and International students. When a student asks about specific fee amounts, ask whether they are a Ghanaian or international student before pointing them to the correct fee schedule/payment info link, since the figures and bank details differ.
