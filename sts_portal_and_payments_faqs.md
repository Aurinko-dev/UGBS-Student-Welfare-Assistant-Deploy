# UG Student Portal, Accounts and Payments (STS FAQs)

Source: sts.ug.edu.gh FAQ page, summarised from screenshots. [Verify against sts.ug.edu.gh and Academic Affairs before treating as final.] Some answers are written for College of Humanities students, including distance learners.

## Account and Access

### Q: How do I reset my MIS Web password?
A: 1. Log in to your UG student email.
2. Log in to MIS Web with your ID number and PIN.
3. Click **Forgot Password**. A temporary password is sent to your student email.
4. Copy the temporary password from your email into the temporary password box on MIS Web.
5. Create a new password following the criteria.
6. Log in.

Password criteria (as far as visible): at least 8 characters, a capital letter, and numbers.

### Q: How do I reset my email password?
A: Go to the University of Ghana Computing Systems (UGCS) Service Desk.

### Q: How can I connect my device to the University Wi-Fi?
A: 1. Turn on Wi-Fi and select the **STUDENT** network.
2. EAP method: **PEAP**.
3. Tap Advanced. Phase 2 authentication can stay on **NONE**. If it does not connect, choose **MSCHAPV2**.
4. CA certificate: if "Please Select" gives other options, choose **Do Not Validate**.
5. Identity/username: your **ID number**. Password: your **5-digit PIN**. Tap Connect.

### Q: Can I view my registered courses and exam results on UGMobile?
A: Yes.
1. Log in with your student ID and PIN.
2. Tap the profile icon on the menu bar, then **Courses and results**.
3. You can see registered courses, grades and the end-of-semester timetable.

### Q: What should I do if I have not received my student ID card?
A: Report to the Academic Affairs Office through your Centre Head.

### Q: I have lost my ID card. How do I request a new one?
A: Students in Accra pay the fee at the main University Cash Office and submit a copy of the original receipt to the Academic Office (COE). Students in other regions contact the Academic Affairs Office through their Centre Heads.

### Q: What should I do if I misplace my student ID card during examinations?
A: Get a provisional one from the Academic Affairs office of the College of Humanities, or from the registration points at the examination centres, for a fee set by the University.

### Q: How do I change my name?
A: The University does not accept requests to change or amend students' names or other records (institutional policy).

## Fees and Payments

### Q: Can I pay for school fees online?
A: Yes, through **UGPay**, the University's web payment platform. It covers tuition, graduation and application fees, and other University services. It accepts Visa and Mastercard issued anywhere. Only UG students with a valid ID number and PIN can use it.

### Q: Can I pay my fees before the start of the semester?
A: Yes.
