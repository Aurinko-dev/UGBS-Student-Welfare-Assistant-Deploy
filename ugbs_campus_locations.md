# University of Ghana — Campus Buildings, Blocks and Lecture Venues

Source: Official UG campus navigation guides, UGBS website, and student-reported locations. [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — confirm unfamiliar locations at UGBS reception or porters desk before a special trip]

## Q: Where are the main lecture blocks on campus?

### N Block (Old N Block)
Used for large undergraduate lectures across multiple colleges. Located in the Social Sciences area of campus. A familiar landmark for most UG students.

### New N Block (NNB)
Located next to Old N Block in the Social Sciences area. Also used for undergraduate lectures. The GCB Building (where SFAO is likely located) is opposite New N Block — so if you are going to Financial Aid, find New N Block first.

### GCB Building
A large white building opposite New N Block. Current likely location of the Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO). Also used for some lectures and administrative offices.

### Commonwealth Hall Area / Mensah Sarbah Area
Traditional halls with their own lecture and study spaces. Students assigned to these halls attend some classes in nearby blocks.

### Lecture Theatre 1, 2, 3 (LT1, LT2, LT3)
Large-capacity lecture theatres used for first and second year courses. LT3 is near CEGENSA and the former SFAO East Legon location.

### UGBS Building Complex
Where all UGBS department offices, the UGBS Registry, Undergraduate Academic Office, K.E. Adjei Library, computer labs, and ICT Lounge are located. GPS: GA-490-9425. Near Volta Hall and UGCS. This is your first stop for any Business Administration programme matter.

### Balme Library
Central campus. The single most useful landmark for orienting yourself on campus. Near the University Square, University Bookshop, University Post Office, and Office of the Dean of Student Affairs.

### JQB Building (John Quarcoo Building)
Near the Main Gate, behind the Institute of African Studies. Ground floor houses the Students Accounts Office. Go here for fee payments, student account issues, and refunds.

### Registry Building
Near the Great Hall / central administration area. The Academic Affairs Directorate (AAD) is in the Left Wing, Room D10. Go here for transcripts, deferment, withdrawal, change of programme, and academic records.

### University Bookshop
On the left side of Balme Library. The Careers and Counselling Directorate has a satellite office at the top of the Bookshop.

### Department of Sociology Building
Behind here is the main office of the Careers and Counselling Directorate. Also near the Languages Department.

### University Post Office
Near Balme Library. The Office of the Dean of Student Affairs is on the same premises.

### Legon Police Station and University Hospital
The Legon Police Station is opposite the University Main Gate. The University Hospital is behind the Police Station.

## Q: Where do UGBS students usually have their lectures?

### Level 100 and 200
Core courses are typically held in large lecture theatres (LT1, LT2, LT3) or N Block / New N Block. Department-specific courses may be held in the UGBS building itself. Check your timetable on SAKAI or the departmental notice board for the exact venue.

### Level 300 and 400
Most lectures move into the UGBS building complex, in department-specific rooms and seminar rooms. Some shared electives may still use central campus lecture theatres.

### Q: How do I confirm my exact lecture venue?
A: Check SAKAI (the university online learning platform) for your course page, or visit the departmental notice board in the UGBS building. You can also check the official examination timetable at https://sts.ug.edu.gh/timetable/ for exam venues.

## Q: Where is the UGBS Library?
A: The K.E. Adjei Library is inside the UGBS building complex. It holds business and management resources. For broader academic databases and research, use Balme Library (central campus) which also provides off-campus access via http://ezproxy.ug.edu.gh/ and past exam papers at http://balme.ug.edu.gh/past.exampapers/

## Q: Where do I pay my fees?
A: Fees can be paid at the Students Accounts Office (JQB Building, Ground Floor) or online via the UG Payments Portal on STS (https://sts.ug.edu.gh). Designated banks on and off campus also accept payments. Keep your receipt and allow 48 hours for your account to be cleared before registering.


## Q: Where do UGBS students have their lectures?
A: Lectures at UGBS are held in the Business School lecture halls and rooms. The main venues are:
- G1, G2, G3 — Ground floor lecture halls in the UGBS building
- A1, A2 — First floor lecture rooms
- B1, B2 — Additional lecture rooms in the UGBS building
For very large classes, the lecturer may propose an alternative venue on the wider UG campus or move the class online. Always check SAKAI or the departmental notice board for your confirmed venue.

## Q: Where do Level 100 students have lectures at UGBS?
A: Level 100 students typically have lectures in the UGBS building halls — G1, G2, G3, A1, A2, B1, B2. For very large introductory courses, lectures may be held in bigger campus venues like the JQB lecture halls or main campus blocks. Check your timetable on SAKAI or the departmental notice board.
