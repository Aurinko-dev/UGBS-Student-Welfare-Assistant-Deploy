# University of Ghana — Careers and Counselling Directorate

## Frequently Asked Questions and Answers

Source: University of Ghana Careers and Counselling Directorate website (ug.edu.gh/careers/faq), accessed August 2026. Where the site's FAQ page did not provide an answer, this is noted below.

### 1. What is counselling and how can it help me?
Counselling is a professional service where a trained counsellor helps you work through thoughts and feelings in a safe, confidential space — for example around stress, anxiety, relationship problems, or feeling overwhelmed. It can help you see your situation from a new angle, build healthy coping skills, improve how you relate to others, and grow your self-confidence.

### 2. What services do you offer?
The Directorate offers individual counselling, couples therapy, group therapy, psychological assessment and testing (e.g. for learning difficulties, ADHD, or personality concerns), crisis intervention for emergencies, and workshops/seminars on topics such as stress management, relationships, and mental health — alongside its career services.

### 3. Where is the centre located?
The main office is on the University of Ghana main campus, beside the Sociology Department and behind the Languages Department. There are also satellite offices at Kingdom Books and Stationery (near Balme Library), Akuafo Hall, Volta Hall, Jubilee Hall, and the City Campus.

### 4. What are the office hours?
8:00am to 5:00pm on weekdays.

### 6. Who can get access to the centre's services?
Students, staff, and individuals outside the University.

### 7. Why might a student want to make use of counselling services?
Counselling supports emotional wellbeing and helps students feel more confident and balanced in their decisions, while career services help with finding the right career path and preparing for work. Together, they support informed, less stressful decisions about the future.

### 8. What is the mode of the counselling session?
Face-to-face sessions are available at all offices; online sessions are also offered for the Takoradi and Cape Coast campuses.

### 9. How can I schedule an appointment?
You can walk in to any of the offices to book an appointment, or book an appointment through the website.

### 10. What types of assessments are available?
Personality and career assessments.

### 11. What should I expect in a counselling session?
A safe, confidential space to talk openly about your concerns — whether stress, mental health, academic, or personal issues. The counsellor listens, gives feedback, and helps you explore your thoughts and feelings, sometimes introducing coping tools such as stress-management strategies. Together you set goals for what you want counselling to achieve, and confidentiality is maintained throughout.
