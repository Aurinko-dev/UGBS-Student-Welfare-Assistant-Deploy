# UGBS and University-Wide Offices: Locations, Contacts, and Who to Ask

This document tells a student roughly WHERE to physically go, WHO to contact, and WHICH office handles what, for the offices mentioned elsewhere in this knowledge base. It merges what used to be several separate, overlapping documents into one, since duplicated location research kept drifting out of sync with itself (see the sourcing note at the end). Locations are the kind of fact that go stale (offices move buildings) and are risky to get wrong, so every entry states its confidence and, where sources disagree, says so rather than picking one. If in doubt, confirm at the UGBS reception/porters' desk or the office's own front desk before making a special trip.

## Section 1: Inside the UGBS building complex

UGBS occupies its own building complex on the Legon campus (GPS: GA-490-9425; P. O. Box LG 78, Legon, Accra; general phone +233 50 158 1492 or +233 50 158 1493; general email ugbsmarcoms@ug.edu.gh; website ugbs.ug.edu.gh, whose Departments menu has a page for each department and whose Contact menu has the contact page), near Volta Hall and the University of Ghana Computing Systems (UGCS) area, per a campus navigation guide. If your question is "what's happening with my Business Administration programme/major/course," start here, not the central Registry. No verified room numbers are available -- ask at the UGBS reception/porters' desk for the specific room. UGBS's own facilities include the K. E. Adjei Library, computer labs, and an ICT Lounge.

## Who is my course advisor?

The Analytics programme has a designated **course advisor** in the OMIS department, introduced to students at orientation. Students in other options should ask their department office who their course advisor is. A course advisor is the right person to talk to about choosing courses and about academic difficulties within an option.

## Can the assistant tell me which office a particular lecturer or course belongs to?

No. The assistant does not hold contact details for individual lecturers, and it does not have a directory of courses. It routes by topic to the responsible administrative office. For a specific lecturer or course, ask the department office that runs the course, or the Academic Office.

## Which office handles laptops and IT support?

The **UGBS IT Unit** runs the **UGBS Laptop Library** and IT support services. Students apply to borrow a laptop, and the unit sets the conditions for borrowing and the duration of use. This is separate from the university-wide Information Technology Directorate (Section 2), which handles UG email, passwords, and campus-wide online systems.

## Which office helps with which welfare matter, at a glance?

- **Academic difficulties, deferment, probation and resits:** Academic Affairs Directorate and your departmental course advisor.
- **Fees and financial aid:** the Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO).
- **Counselling and emotional support:** the Counselling and Placement Centre (UGCCD) -- see the note under Careers and Counselling Directorate (Section 2) about the unresolved relationship between these two.
- **Sexual harassment support:** CEGENSA and the Anti-Sexual Harassment Committee.
- **Student representation:** the BHJCR (see the BHJCR constitution) and the SRC.
- **Careers and internships:** the Counselling and Placement Centre, and student associations such as the Business Analytics Students Association (BASA) for analytics students.

## Section 2: University-wide offices (not inside UGBS)

### Academic Affairs Directorate (AAD)
**Location:** Room D10, Left Wing of the Registry, University of Ghana (close to the Great Hall).
**Confidence:** Single detailed source giving the room number; the general "Left Wing of the Registry" location is corroborated by how the Registry itself is described (see below). This is one of the most important offices for a UGBS student and is referenced throughout this knowledge base, but had no location recorded until now.
**Go here for:** academic records, transcripts, certificates, attestation, academic verification, deferment, withdrawal, change of programme, re-marking/grade-change procedures, repetition, and academic registration issues.
**Emails:** general aad@ug.edu.gh; student records academic@ug.edu.gh; admissions admissions@ug.edu.gh; teaching/examinations teu-academic@ug.edu.gh.

### University Registry
**Location:** Near the Great Hall / central administration area. The Academic Affairs Directorate is specifically the left wing of this same complex.
**Confidence:** UNVERIFIED -- a second, independent attempt to confirm this against an official UG source also failed. Treat as a plausible lead, not a confirmed fact, and suggest the student confirm on arrival or via the Academic Affairs Directorate directly.
**Use for:** university-level administration -- matters bigger than UGBS that concern your official status as a UG student. This is the UNIVERSITY registry, separate from the UGBS Registry in Section 1.

### Students Accounts Office
**Location:** JQB Building, Ground Floor, Main Campus, Legon.
**Confidence:** Two independent sources agree on JQB Building, Ground Floor -- treated as reliable.
**Phone:** sources disagree and this is unresolved -- one source gives 020 513 6904, another gives +233 209 114 001. Confirm before calling, or use email: studentsaccounts@ug.edu.gh. Payment-challenge hours are reported as 9:00-16:00 GMT.
**Additional caveat:** a second, independently compiled guide could also not confirm JQB's exact location/floor beyond the original submitted description -- treat the JQB/ground-floor detail as an unverified lead, and suggest the student confirm the exact office/floor on arrival.
**Go here for:** school fees, student account problems, refunds, payment issues, fee balances, and questions about money credited/debited to your student account.
**Important distinction:** this is NOT the same as the UGBS Bursary (Section 1). If the issue is your central UG student account or fees specifically, use Students Accounts; for a UGBS-internal financial/administrative matter, use the UGBS Bursary.

### Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO)
**Location: likely RELOCATED -- treat GCB Building as current.** Several official University of Ghana pages from 2021-2024 describe SFAO as "No. 11 East Legon, behind the College of Basic and Applied Sciences (CBAS), opposite CEGENSA and behind LT3 Lecture Hall." More recent sources place it at the **GCB Building, Main Campus** -- a large white building opposite New N Block -- and explicitly frame this as the current location, noting it is preferable to rely on this over older references to "the former location." This wording suggests SFAO moved buildings at some point, which would explain the discrepancy, but no explicit relocation announcement has been found to confirm this outright. **Treat GCB Building (opposite New N Block) as the current location, and confirm on arrival or by phone if making a special trip**, since the CEGENSA/CBAS description may be outdated rather than wrong.
**Route from Balme Library:** head toward the Social Sciences / N Block side, find New N Block, and GCB Building is the large building opposite it.
**Phone: UNRESOLVED -- at least three different numbers have appeared across sources**, and none has been confirmed as definitively current: 030 299 8686 (two independent recent sources), 0302-945312 (a separately compiled and cross-checked guide), and the older official set 020 518 6904 / 0206804077 / 0303-965240. Do not present any single one of these to a student as confirmed correct -- use email (finaid@ug.edu.gh) as the more reliable fallback, or say the phone number could not be confirmed.
**Go here for:** financial aid, scholarships, "needy-but-brilliant" support, and scholarship enquiries -- including UGBS-specific schemes such as the UGBS/AXIS Pension Scholarship, which are administered through SFAO.

### CEGENSA (Centre for Gender Studies and Advocacy)
**Location:** Described as opposite SFAO's East Legon location, close to LT3 Lecture Theatre. **Caveat:** since SFAO's current location may actually be the GCB Building (see SFAO entry above), this "opposite SFAO" description may itself refer to SFAO's FORMER location, not where SFAO is now. CEGENSA's own building may not have moved even if SFAO's has -- but this relative description should be re-verified, not assumed current.
**Confidence:** the "opposite SFAO, near LT3" description is corroborated by two independent sources, but both predate the likely SFAO relocation discussed above.
**Handles:** sexual harassment and misconduct reports, both informal and formal.

### Careers and Counselling Directorate
**Location:** Main office behind the Department of Sociology (also described as beside Sociology and behind the Languages Department).
**Confidence:** VERIFIED -- two independent descriptions agree on this location.
**Phone:** three numbers reported across sources: 020 499 9221, 024 594 5752, and 0302-213820 (this last one cited directly from the Directorate's official contact page in a separately compiled guide -- possibly the most reliable of the three, but not confirmed against the other two).
**Email:** careers@ug.edu.gh.
**Go here for:** personal counselling, academic-related support, student wellbeing support, career guidance, CV support, career planning, and internship/job guidance.
**Satellite offices:** sources differ on the full list. One source names the top of the University of Ghana Bookshop; another (already in careers_and_counselling_faq.md) names Kingdom Books and Stationery near Balme Library, plus Akuafo Hall, Volta Hall, Jubilee Hall, and the City Campus. Both may be accurate for different points in time or different satellite locations -- treat the list of satellites as incomplete rather than final.
**Note:** if the student's need is personal/mental-health counselling specifically, see also ugccd counselling policy.md, which describes a separate Counselling and Placement Centre (UGCCD) with its own, SIMULATED/unverified location near Balme Library. This knowledge base has not resolved whether UGCCD and the Careers and Counselling Directorate are the same body, related bodies, or distinct offices -- this is a genuine open question and should be verified before real-world use.

### Office of the Dean of Student Affairs
**Location:** Same premises as the University Post Office, near Balme Library.
**Confidence:** Single source.
**Go here for:** student welfare, student affairs, student-life issues, student organisations/activities, and certain disciplinary/student matters. Distinct from your academic department.

### Balme Library
**Location:** Central/Main Campus. A major landmark for navigating campus, and near the University Post Office / Dean of Student Affairs.
**Use for:** books, journals, academic databases, research, and quiet study.

### University Hospital and University of Ghana Medical Centre (UGMC)
**These are two different facilities -- do not confuse them (a source consulted for this document made exactly this error, reusing one listing's details for both; that duplication has been discarded here).**
- **University Hospital, Legon:** near/behind the Legon Police Station, which is itself opposite the University Main Gate. Phone: 030 703 0184.
- **University of Ghana Medical Centre (UGMC):** UGMC's own site describes its general area as "the southernmost tip of the University of Ghana" (this specific phrase is treated as more reliable, since it comes from UGMC's own site). It has also been described as being behind the Diaspora Halls, but this specific relational detail could not be independently confirmed -- treat it as an unverified lead, not a confirmed fact, and point a student to UGMC's own site/contact for exact directions if precision matters. Phone: 030 255 0843 (from an earlier, separately verified source).
**Use for:** general medical care, consultations, emergencies, and student health services. If a message signals a medical emergency, this is where a student should be directed alongside any relevant campus emergency contacts -- this is separate from the AI system's own crisis-detection and escalation process for welfare/mental-health messages (see risk_classifier.py and ugccd counselling policy.md).

### Information Technology Directorate
**Confidence:** Listed among the University's central administration units in the University Statutes.
**Use for:** UG student email, password/account problems, online university systems, network/Wi-Fi issues, and other technology services. For a programme-specific online academic issue, try UGBS or Academic Affairs first.

### International Programmes Office
**Location: UNVERIFIED.** One submitted guide places it opposite the Law School, on the Pent Evandy stretch; a separate source gives Annie Jiagge Road, Main Campus. Neither has been independently confirmed against an official UG source, and it is unclear whether these describe the same location differently or are in tension. Confirm before a special trip.
**Use for:** international student programmes, exchange opportunities, study abroad, and international partnerships.

### Everyday campus services
Postal services, banking, shops, copy centres, eateries, sports facilities, and Wi-Fi are all available on campus, alongside the medical and careers/counselling services listed above -- students do not necessarily need to leave campus for common everyday needs.

## Section 3: Campus navigation -- walking between these offices

This section is landmark-to-landmark walking guidance, not a scale map -- the exact path depends on which campus road or pedestrian route is used, and it should be treated as a starting orientation, not a guarantee.

### The 8 landmarks worth memorising first

| Landmark | Helps you find |
|---|---|
| Main Gate | JQB, Legon Police Station, direction to University Hospital |
| JQB | Students Accounts and other academic-related services |
| Registry | Academic Affairs Directorate (left wing) and central administration |
| Balme Library | Central campus reference point -- probably the single most useful "you are here" landmark |
| University Square | Central orientation point, next to Balme Library |
| University Bookshop | Careers and Counselling satellite office (at the top of the Bookshop) |
| N Block / New N Block | Landmark for finding the GCB Building / Financial Aid |
| Department of Sociology | Careers and Counselling Directorate's main office |

### Useful routes

- **UGBS to Registry / Balme Library:** UGBS -> Volta Hall -> University Square -> Registry (for Academic Affairs) or Balme Library.
- **Registry to JQB (Students Accounts):** Registry -> toward the Main Gate direction -> past the Institute of African Studies -> JQB (JQB is close to the Main Entrance, behind the Institute of African Studies, on the right when approaching from the Main Gate).
- **Balme Library to Careers and Counselling:** Balme -> toward the Sociology/Languages area -> behind the Department of Sociology. Alternatively, Balme -> University Bookshop (on the left side of Balme) -> top of the Bookshop, for the satellite office.
- **Balme Library to SFAO (Financial Aid):** Balme -> Social Sciences -> N Block -> New N Block -> GCB Building is the large building opposite New N Block. (See the caveat under SFAO above about this being the likely-current, not certainly-confirmed, location.)
- **Main Gate to University Hospital:** Main Gate -> locate the Legon Police Station (opposite the Main Gate) -> the Hospital is behind the Police Station.
- **Balme Library to Student Affairs:** Balme -> University Post Office -> Office of the Dean of Student Affairs (same premises as the Post Office).
- **International Programmes Office:** opposite the Law School, on the Pent Evandy stretch.

## How this document was built

Section 1 (offices inside UGBS) is based on the confirmed six-department structure (see ugbs_programmes_and_options.md's sourcing note). Section 2 combines the team's own campus-directory research with independent web searches of official University of Ghana pages. Entries marked VERIFIED had at least two independent sources in agreement; entries marked UNRESOLVED or UNVERIFIED have genuinely conflicting or unconfirmed information across multiple independent research attempts, and are stated honestly as such rather than picked arbitrarily. This document has now been assembled from THREE separate rounds of location research (the team's own, and two further independently compiled guides), which is why several entries -- SFAO's phone number, JQB's exact floor, the Registry/Academic Affairs wing arrangement, the International Programmes Office's street, and the relationship between the Careers and Counselling Directorate and UGCCD -- remain open questions even after repeated attempts to confirm them. That a second or third independent attempt could not resolve these is itself useful information: it suggests these details either are not published anywhere official, or change often enough that no single answer stays correct for long. Room numbers are omitted where no source confirms them. This information should be treated as a starting point for navigating campus, not a guarantee, and re-verified before any real-world deployment.
